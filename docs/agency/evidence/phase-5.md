## Result and implementation

- Dashboard authority is fetched as one request-local current membership/grant/client/channel scope. Group target/receipt facts and materialize classification before scoped filters/counts/pagination. Query-local `SET LOCAL jit = off` avoids compiler overhead without changing pooled/database-wide settings. Ownership, channel allowlists, revocation and historical uncertainty assertions remain intact.
- Provider post/comment/finalize helpers cannot blindly repeat ambiguous HTTP/streamed mutations inside a claim. SDK/platform errors are redacted before persistence; Threads no longer logs raw error bodies. Read-only checks retain their retry policy and redact failures.
- A terminal SDK rejection alone cannot establish FAILED. A bounded positive read-only no-side-effect contract must prove it for the current persisted attempt; otherwise keep UNKNOWN. A real synthetic acceptance followed by BadBody stays unknown, cannot retry, and reconciles without another mutation. Inherited live providers have no opt-in proof.
- Matching same-attempt completion is idempotent after confirmed reconciliation. Stale attempts/conflicting receipt identities remain denied; late failure cannot append a false unknown audit. TikTok inbox UPLOAD is denied for agency publication, and an inbox delivery cannot count as a confirmed publication.
- Existing cookie-origin protection is retained; hostile browser origins are also rejected for explicit staff/API headers and login. Remove URL-carried session intake. Invitations accept a bounded single ASCII mailbox, excluding list/comment/display-name ambiguity. Review times include the UTC offset, distinguishing repeated DST hours while keeping the exact immutable UTC time/hash.
- Apply targeted HTTP/media/editor/transport security patches and align ProseMirror model 1.25.12 with patched view types. Upstream pin, AGPL notices, migration files and all workflow source remain unchanged.
